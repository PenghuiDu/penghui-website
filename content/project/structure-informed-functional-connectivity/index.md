Functional connectivity is constrained, but not determined, by the brain's
structural wiring. This project treated fMRI activity as a signal living on the
structural connectome graph and used **statistical signal analysis on graphs** to
characterize structure-informed functional connectivity — separating the component
of functional coupling that is explained by structural eigenmodes from the
component that is not.

Semester project at the [MIP Lab](https://miplab.epfl.ch), EPFL, supervised by
Michael Chan and Prof. Dimitri Van De Ville.
