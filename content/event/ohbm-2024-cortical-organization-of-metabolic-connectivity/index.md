abstract: 'Poster #1496, presented at the 2024 OHBM Annual Meeting. Using a
  resting-state simultaneous fPET-fMRI dataset, we characterized the detailed
  cortical organization of fPET-derived metabolic connectivity (MC) and how it
  differs from fMRI-derived network structure. MC showed robust spatial features
  deviating from those of functional connectivity, was dominated by low-frequency
  components, and was characterized by prominent fronto-parietal and inferior
  temporal-occipital components. We also examined mechanisms, such as imperfect
  baseline removal and shared scanning experience across subjects, that may give
  rise to apparent MC.'
